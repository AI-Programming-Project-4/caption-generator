You are creating training captions for Qwen-Image, a text-to-image generation model.

Given a scientific figure image, its original paper caption, OCR words, and metadata, write a descriptive caption that would help an image generation model reproduce the figure.

The caption should describe:
- the type of figure,
- the visual structure,
- the layout,
- the main components,
- the use of arrows, boxes, panels, or embedded images,
- and the academic paper figure style.

Rules:
- Write 1 or 2 clear sentences.
- Do not simply copy the original caption.
- Expand the original caption into a useful image-generation prompt.
- Use important OCR words only if they are visible and relevant.
- Do not hallucinate content that is not visible.
- Do not mention exact image file names.
- Do not include "Figure".
- Output only the training caption.
